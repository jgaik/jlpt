# A. Verb-stem patterns

## 1. ～始める・～終わる・～続ける

**Meaning:** Begin, finish, or continue an action.

**Form:**  
Vます-stem + 始める / 終わる / 続ける

**Usage:**
- 始める: an action begins
- 終わる: an action is completed
- 続ける: an action continues

**Examples:**

<details>
<summary>雨が降り始めた。</summary>

- Reading: あめがふりはじめた
- Translation: It started raining.
- Nuance: 始める focuses on the beginning of an action.
</details>

<details>
<summary>宿題をやり終わった。</summary>

- Reading: しゅくだいをやりおわった
- Translation: I finished my homework.
- Nuance: 終わる indicates that the action has reached its completion.
</details>

<details>
<summary>彼は3時間も走り続けた。</summary>

- Reading: かれはさんじかんもはしりつづけた
- Translation: He kept running for as long as three hours.
- Nuance: 続ける emphasizes the continuation of an action. も highlights the surprisingly long duration.
</details>

Related: [[～出す]], [[～かける]], [[～っぱなし]]

---

## 2. ～そうもない・～そうにない・～そうにもない

**Meaning:** It doesn't seem likely that something will happen.

**Form:**  
Vます-stem + そうもない / そうにない / そうにもない

**Usage:**
- Expresses a negative prediction based on what the speaker sees or expects.
- Often used when something seems unlikely to happen soon or at all.
- そうにもない can add emphasis to the negative impression.

**Examples:**

<details>
<summary>雨は止みそうにない。</summary>

- Reading: あめはやみそうにない
- Translation: It doesn't look like the rain will stop.
- Nuance: The speaker judges from the current situation that the rain is unlikely to stop.
</details>

<details>
<summary>この量は一日では食べ終わりそうもない。</summary>

- Reading: このりょうはいちにちではたべおわりそうもない
- Translation: It doesn't seem possible to finish eating this amount in one day.
- Nuance: そうもない expresses a strong negative expectation.
</details>

<details>
<summary>彼はまだ帰ってきそうにもない。</summary>

- Reading: かれはまだかえってきそうにもない
- Translation: It doesn't look like he'll be back anytime soon.
- Nuance: まだ and にもない reinforce the impression that his return is not imminent.
</details>

Related: [[～そうだ]], [[～らしい]], [[～はずがない]]

---

## 3. ～きる・～きれない

**Meaning:** Do something completely; be unable to finish or do something completely.

**Form:**  
Vます-stem + きる / きれない

**Usage:**
- きる: complete an action fully, or do it to the very end.
- きれない: cannot complete the action, often because there is too much of it.
- Can also express an extreme degree, as in 感謝してもしきれない.

**Examples:**

<details>
<summary>マラソンを最後まで走りきった。</summary>

- Reading: マラソンをさいごまではしりきった
- Translation: I ran the marathon all the way to the end.
- Nuance: きる emphasizes completing the action despite its difficulty or length.
</details>

<details>
<summary>一日では読みきれないほど本がある。</summary>

- Reading: いちにちではよみきれないほどほんがある
- Translation: There are so many books that I can't finish reading them in one day.
- Nuance: きれない indicates that the quantity exceeds the speaker's ability to complete the action.
</details>

<details>
<summary>感謝してもしきれない。</summary>

- Reading: かんしゃしてもしきれない
- Translation: I can never express enough gratitude.
- Nuance: A common expression meaning that one's gratitude is too great to fully express.
</details>

Related: [[～終わる]], [[～っこない]], [[～かける]]

---

## 4. ～かける・～かけだ・～かけて・～かけた・～かけのN

**Meaning:** Have started something but not finished it; be in the middle of starting something.

**Form:**  
Vます-stem + かける

Common forms:
- ～かける
- ～かけだ
- ～かけて
- ～かけた
- ～かけの + N

**Usage:**
- Describes an action that has begun but remains unfinished.
- Often implies that the action was interrupted or abandoned.
- ～かけのN describes something left partially done.

**Examples:**

<details>
<summary>読みかけの本を机に置いた。</summary>

- Reading: よみかけのほんをつくえにおいた
- Translation: I put the half-read book on the desk.
- Nuance: 読みかけの本 is a book that someone started reading but hasn't finished.
</details>

<details>
<summary>ご飯を食べかけたとき、電話が鳴った。</summary>

- Reading: ごはんをたべかけたとき、でんわがなった
- Translation: Just as I had started eating, the phone rang.
- Nuance: 食べかけた indicates that the action had just begun when something interrupted it.
</details>

<details>
<summary>彼は何か言いかけて、黙ってしまった。</summary>

- Reading: かれはなにかいいかけて、だまってしまった
- Translation: He started to say something, then fell silent.
- Nuance: 言いかけて suggests that he began speaking but did not finish what he intended to say.
</details>

Related: [[～始める]], [[～終わる]], [[～っぱなし]]

---

## 5. ～っこない

**Meaning:** There's no way something will happen; someone absolutely cannot or will not do something.

**Form:**  
Vます-stem + っこない

**Usage:**
- Expresses a strong, often subjective conviction that something is impossible or extremely unlikely.
- Common in casual conversation.
- Stronger and more emphatic than a neutral negative prediction.

**Examples:**

<details>
<summary>そんな難しい問題、私には解けっこない。</summary>

- Reading: そんなむずかしいもんだい、わたしにはとけっこない
- Translation: There's no way I can solve such a difficult problem.
- Nuance: The speaker feels completely incapable of solving it.
</details>

<details>
<summary>今から走っても、電車に間に合いっこない。</summary>

- Reading: いまからはしっても、でんしゃにまにあいっこない
- Translation: Even if I run now, there's no way I'll make the train.
- Nuance: っこない expresses the speaker's emphatic certainty that it is impossible.
</details>

<details>
<summary>彼がそんなことをするっこない。</summary>

- Reading: かれがそんなことをするっこない
- Translation: There's no way he'd do such a thing.
- Nuance: The speaker strongly rejects the possibility that he would do it.
</details>

Related: [[～そうにない]], [[～はずがない・～わけがない]], [[～きれない]]

---

## 6. ～っぱなし

**Meaning:** Leave something in a certain state; keep doing something without stopping.

**Form:**  
Vます-stem + っぱなし

**Usage:**
- Describes an action that continues without interruption.
- Often indicates that something has been left undone or neglected.
- Frequently carries a negative or critical nuance.

**Examples:**

<details>
<summary>電気をつけっぱなしで出かけてしまった。</summary>

- Reading: でんきをつけっぱなしででかけてしまった
- Translation: I went out and left the lights on.
- Nuance: The lights remained on because the speaker forgot to turn them off.
</details>

<details>
<summary>彼は一日中しゃべりっぱなしだった。</summary>

- Reading: かれはいちにちじゅうしゃべりっぱなしだった
- Translation: He talked nonstop all day.
- Nuance: The action continued for a long time without a break.
</details>

<details>
<summary>窓を開けっぱなしにしないで。</summary>

- Reading: まどをあけっぱなしにしないで
- Translation: Don't leave the window open.
- Nuance: ～っぱなしにする describes leaving something in a particular state.
</details>

Related: [[～続ける]], [[～かける]], [[～まま]]

---

## 7. ～っぽい

**Meaning:** -ish; -like; tends to do something.

**Form:**  
N / Vます-stem / adjective stem + っぽい

**Usage:**
- With nouns: something resembles or has the qualities of that noun.
- With verb stems: someone tends to do something.
- With some adjective stems: describes a noticeable quality or tendency.
- Often conveys an informal impression.

**Examples:**

<details>
<summary>この服は子供っぽい。</summary>

- Reading: このふくはこどもっぽい
- Translation: These clothes look childish.
- Nuance: っぽい suggests that something has the qualities associated with children, often in a slightly critical way.
</details>

<details>
<summary>彼は忘れっぽい。</summary>

- Reading: かれはわすれっぽい
- Translation: He's forgetful.
- Nuance: The verb stem 忘れ combined with っぽい describes a habitual tendency.
</details>

<details>
<summary>今日は油っぽい料理を食べたくない。</summary>

- Reading: きょうはあぶらっぽいりょうりをたべたくない
- Translation: I don't want to eat oily food today.
- Nuance: 油っぽい means that the food seems greasy or contains a noticeable amount of oil.
</details>

Related: [[～らしい]], [[～みたい]], [[～がち]]

---

## 8. ～ぎみ

**Meaning:** Slightly; showing signs of; tending to be in a certain state.

**Form:**  
N / Vます-stem + ぎみ

**Usage:**
- Describes a slight tendency or condition.
- Commonly used for temporary physical or emotional states.
- Often used with negative conditions such as tiredness, illness, or delays.

**Examples:**

<details>
<summary>最近、疲れぎみです。</summary>

- Reading: さいきん、つかれぎみです
- Translation: I've been feeling a little tired lately.
- Nuance: 疲れぎみ describes a noticeable but not necessarily severe state of tiredness.
</details>

<details>
<summary>この電車は遅れぎみだ。</summary>

- Reading: このでんしゃはおくれぎみだ
- Translation: This train tends to be a little late.
- Nuance: 遅れぎみ suggests that delays are occurring somewhat more often or than expected.
</details>

<details>
<summary>風邪ぎみなので、今日は早く寝ます。</summary>

- Reading: かぜぎみなので、きょうははやくねます
- Translation: I'm feeling a little under the weather, so I'll go to bed early today.
- Nuance: 風邪ぎみ means showing slight symptoms of a cold.
</details>

Related: [[～っぽい]], [[～がち]], [[～気味]]

---

## 9. ～がる・～がり

**Meaning:** Show signs of feeling or wanting something.

**Form:**  
い-adjective without い + がる  
な-adjective stem + がる  
欲しい → 欲しがる

Noun + がり + N

**Usage:**
- Used to describe another person's visible or expressed feelings and desires.
- The speaker observes the feeling from the outside.
- ～がり describes someone who tends to feel or behave in a certain way.

**Examples:**

<details>
<summary>子供は新しいおもちゃを欲しがっている。</summary>

- Reading: こどもはあたらしいおもちゃをほしがっている
- Translation: The child wants a new toy.
- Nuance: 欲しがる describes the child's desire as observed by the speaker.
</details>

<details>
<summary>彼は人前で話すのを嫌がる。</summary>

- Reading: かれはひとまえではなすのをいやがる
- Translation: He dislikes speaking in front of people.
- Nuance: 嫌がる means that he shows reluctance or displeasure.
</details>

<details>
<summary>妹は怖がりだ。</summary>

- Reading: いもうとはこわがりだ
- Translation: My younger sister is easily frightened.
- Nuance: 怖がり is a noun describing someone who tends to be afraid.
</details>

Related: [[～そうだ]], [[～っぽい]], [[～たがる]]

---

## 10. ～ようとする

**Meaning:** Try to do something; be about to do something.

**Form:**  
Vよう + とする

**Usage:**
- Describes an attempt to perform an action.
- Can describe an action that is just about to happen.
- Often used with としたとき, としたら, or としても.

**Examples:**

<details>
<summary>ドアを開けようとしたが、鍵がかかっていた。</summary>

- Reading: ドアをあけようとしたが、かぎがかかっていた
- Translation: I tried to open the door, but it was locked.
- Nuance: 開けようとした describes an attempt that did not succeed.
</details>

<details>
<summary>出かけようとしたとき、雨が降り始めた。</summary>

- Reading: でかけようとしたとき、あめがふりはじめた
- Translation: Just as I was about to go out, it started raining.
- Nuance: ～ようとしたとき marks the moment immediately before an action.
</details>

<details>
<summary>何度説明しようとしても、うまく伝わらない。</summary>

- Reading: なんどせつめいしようとしても、うまくつたわらない
- Translation: No matter how many times I try to explain, it doesn't get across.
- Nuance: ～ようとしても expresses repeated attempts that do not achieve the desired result.
</details>

Related: [[～かける]], [[～ために]], [[～ようとするところ]]

---

## 11. ～させてもらう・～させてくれる

**Meaning:** Be allowed to do something; let someone do something.

**Form:**  
Vさせて + もらう / くれる

**Usage:**
- ～させてもらう: the speaker receives permission or an opportunity to do something.
- ～させてくれる: someone grants the speaker permission or allows them to do something.
- もらう focuses on receiving the favor; くれる focuses on the other person's action of granting it.

**Examples:**

<details>
<summary>今日は早く帰らせてもらいます。</summary>

- Reading: きょうははやくかえらせてもらいます
- Translation: I'll be allowed to go home early today.
- Nuance: The speaker presents leaving early as something permitted by another person.
</details>

<details>
<summary>先生は私に発表させてくれた。</summary>

- Reading: せんせいはわたしにはっぴょうさせてくれた
- Translation: The teacher let me give the presentation.
- Nuance: くれる emphasizes the teacher granting the opportunity to the speaker.
</details>

<details>
<summary>少し考えさせてもらえませんか。</summary>

- Reading: すこしかんがえさせてもらえませんか
- Translation: Could you give me a little time to think?
- Nuance: A polite request for permission or time to do something.
</details>

Related: [[～させていただく]], [[～てもらう・～てくれる]], [[使役形]]

---

## 12. ～させておく

**Meaning:** Let someone do something; have someone do something in advance or leave them doing it.

**Form:**  
Vさせて + おく

**Usage:**
- Allows or permits someone to perform an action.
- Can express letting someone do something without interfering.
- おく may add the meaning of doing something in preparation or leaving a situation as it is.

**Examples:**

<details>
<summary>子供には好きなことをさせておこう。</summary>

- Reading: こどもにはすきなことをさせておこう
- Translation: Let's let the children do what they like.
- Nuance: させておこう expresses a decision to allow the action and leave things that way.
</details>

<details>
<summary>彼を一人で考えさせておいた。</summary>

- Reading: かれをひとりでかんがえさせておいた
- Translation: I left him to think by himself.
- Nuance: The speaker deliberately did not interfere and allowed him to continue thinking.
</details>

<details>
<summary>発表の前に、学生に練習させておく。</summary>

- Reading: はっぴょうのまえに、がくせいにれんしゅうさせておく
- Translation: I'll have the students practise before the presentation.
- Nuance: ～ておく indicates preparation for a later event.
</details>

Related: [[～ておく]], [[～させてもらう]], [[使役形]]

---

## 13. ～させる・～させられる

**Meaning:** Make or let someone do something; be made or forced to do something.

**Form:**  
使役形: Vさせる  
使役受身形: Vさせられる

**Usage:**
- させる: makes or allows another person to perform an action.
- させられる: the subject is made or forced to perform an action.
- The context determines whether させる means permission or coercion.

**Examples:**

<details>
<summary>母は私に野菜を食べさせた。</summary>

- Reading: はははわたしにやさいをたべさせた
- Translation: My mother made me eat vegetables.
- Nuance: The mother caused or required the speaker to eat them.
</details>

<details>
<summary>先生は学生を自由に発表させた。</summary>

- Reading: せんせいはがくせいをじゆうにはっぴょうさせた
- Translation: The teacher allowed the students to present freely.
- Nuance: Here, させる expresses permission rather than force.
</details>

<details>
<summary>私は毎日残業させられている。</summary>

- Reading: わたしはまいにちざんぎょうさせられている
- Translation: I'm being made to work overtime every day.
- Nuance: させられる expresses an action imposed on the subject, often against their wishes.
</details>

Related: [[～させてもらう]], [[～させておく]], [[受身形]]

---

## 14. ～させていただく・～させてくださる

**Meaning:** Humbly receive permission to do something; graciously allow someone to do something.

**Form:**  
Vさせて + いただく / くださる

**Usage:**
- ～させていただく: the speaker humbly receives permission or a favor.
- ～させてくださる: someone respectfully grants permission to the speaker.
- These forms are more polite than ～させてもらう and ～させてくれる.

**Examples:**

<details>
<summary>本日は休ませていただきます。</summary>

- Reading: ほんじつはやすませていただきます
- Translation: I will take the day off today.
- Nuance: A polite way to communicate that the speaker is taking leave with permission or consideration from others.
</details>

<details>
<summary>社長は私に説明させてくださいました。</summary>

- Reading: しゃちょうはわたしにせつめいさせてくださいました
- Translation: The president kindly allowed me to explain.
- Nuance: くださる respectfully describes the other person's granting of permission.
</details>

<details>
<summary>この場を借りて、お礼を言わせていただきます。</summary>

- Reading: このばをかりて、おれいをいわせていただきます
- Translation: Allow me to take this opportunity to express my thanks.
- Nuance: A formal expression used when respectfully asking to perform an action.
</details>

Related: [[～させてもらう・～させてくれる]], [[敬語]], [[使役形]]

# B. Verb plain form

## ～ように言う・注意する・頼む

### Form

- Vる + ように + 言う
- Vない + ように + 言う
- Vる / Vない + ように + 注意する・頼む・伝える...

### Meaning / nuance

Tell, warn, ask, or instruct someone to do or not do something.

### Usage

Used when reporting instructions, requests, or warnings.

The person receiving the instruction is often marked with **に**.

### Examples

<details>
<summary>先生は学生に遅刻しないように言った。</summary>

**Reading:** せんせいはがくせいにちこくしないようにいった。

**Translation:** The teacher told the students not to be late.

The instruction is expressed indirectly using ように.
</details>

<details>
<summary>医者にお酒を飲みすぎないように注意された。</summary>

**Reading:** いしゃにおさけをのみすぎないようにちゅういされた。

**Translation:** The doctor warned me not to drink too much alcohol.

注意する expresses a warning or advice.
</details>

<details>
<summary>友達に駅で待つように頼んだ。</summary>

**Reading:** ともだちにえきでまつようにたのんだ。

**Translation:** I asked my friend to wait at the station.

頼む expresses a request.
</details>

### Related grammar

- [[～てください]] — direct request
- [[～ようにする]] — make an effort to do something
- [[～ことにする]] — decide to do something

---

## ～と

### Form

- Vる + と

### Meaning / nuance

When / whenever something happens, another result follows naturally or automatically.

### Usage

Used for:
- Natural consequences
- Automatic results
- Directions or instructions
- Discoveries made after doing something

The result clause generally describes something that happens naturally, rather than a deliberate action or request by the speaker.

### Examples

<details>
<summary>このボタンを押すと、ドアが開きます。</summary>

**Reading:** このボタンをおすと、ドアがひらきます。

**Translation:** When you press this button, the door opens.

The result happens automatically.
</details>

<details>
<summary>春になると、桜が咲く。</summary>

**Reading:** はるになると、さくらがさく。

**Translation:** When spring comes, cherry blossoms bloom.

と describes a natural or recurring result.
</details>

<details>
<summary>窓を開けると、富士山が見えた。</summary>

**Reading:** まどをあけると、ふじさんがみえた。

**Translation:** When I opened the window, I saw Mount Fuji.

The second clause describes a discovery.
</details>

### Related grammar

- [[～たら]] — if / when
- [[～ば]] — if
- [[～とき]] — when

---

## ～たら

### Form

- Vた + ら

### Meaning / nuance

If / when something happens or has happened.

### Usage

Used for:
- Conditions
- Hypothetical situations
- Discoveries after an action
- Actions that follow another action

Unlike ～と, the result can describe the speaker's intention, request, or decision.

### Examples

<details>
<summary>時間があったら、映画を見に行きたい。</summary>

**Reading:** じかんがあったら、えいがをみにいきたい。

**Translation:** If I have time, I want to go see a movie.

たら expresses a condition.
</details>

<details>
<summary>家に帰ったら、誰もいなかった。</summary>

**Reading:** いえにかえったら、だれもいなかった。

**Translation:** When I got home, nobody was there.

The speaker discovered something after arriving.
</details>

<details>
<summary>駅に着いたら、電話してください。</summary>

**Reading:** えきについたら、でんわしてください。

**Translation:** Please call me when you arrive at the station.

たら can introduce a request.
</details>

### Related grammar

- [[～と]] — natural or automatic result
- [[～ば]] — conditional
- [[～なら]] — if / given that

---

## ～ことがある・～こともある

### Form

- Vる + ことがある
- Vる + こともある

### Meaning / nuance

Sometimes do something; there are occasions when something happens.

### Usage

Used to describe occasional experiences or events.

- ことがある: sometimes / there are occasions when
- こともある: sometimes too / there are also occasions when

This pattern is different from **Vたことがある**, which means having experienced something in the past.

### Examples

<details>
<summary>仕事のあと、友達と食事することがある。</summary>

**Reading:** しごとのあと、ともだちとしょくじすることがある。

**Translation:** Sometimes I have dinner with friends after work.

Describes an occasional habit.
</details>

<details>
<summary>忙しいと、昼ご飯を食べないこともある。</summary>

**Reading:** いそがしいと、ひるごはんをたべないこともある。

**Translation:** When I'm busy, sometimes I don't eat lunch.

も highlights that this is one possible occurrence.
</details>

<details>
<summary>休日でも、会社に行くことがある。</summary>

**Reading:** きゅうじつでも、かいしゃにいくことがある。

**Translation:** Sometimes I go to the office even on holidays.

ことがある describes something that happens occasionally.
</details>

### Related grammar

- [[Vたことがある]] — have experienced something
- [[～たり～たりする]] — do things such as...
- [[～場合がある]] — there are cases when

---

## ～たりして

### Form

- Vた + りして

### Meaning / nuance

Do things such as...; sometimes do something unexpected or surprising.

### Usage

Used to:
- Give an example of an action
- Mention an action casually
- Describe an unexpected or surprising behavior

Often appears in casual speech.

### Examples

<details>
<summary>休日は映画を見たりして過ごします。</summary>

**Reading:** きゅうじつはえいがをみたりしてすごします。

**Translation:** I spend my days off doing things like watching movies.

たり gives an example of an activity.
</details>

<details>
<summary>彼は急に怒ったりして、びっくりした。</summary>

**Reading:** かれはきゅうにおこったりして、びっくりした。

**Translation:** He suddenly got angry and surprised me.

たりして presents an unexpected action.
</details>

<details>
<summary>そんなことを言ったりして、失礼だよ。</summary>

**Reading:** そんなことをいったりして、しつれいだよ。

**Translation:** Saying something like that is rude.

たりして can soften or generalize a criticism.
</details>

### Related grammar

- [[～たり～たりする]] — list examples of actions
- [[～なんか]] — something like / such as

## ～くせに

### Form

- な-adjective + な + くせに
- N + の + くせに
- Plain form + くせに

### Meaning / nuance

- **Even though… / despite…**
- Expresses criticism, dissatisfaction, or a contradiction between someone's situation and behavior.

### Usage

- Usually used when the speaker feels that the result or behavior is unreasonable.
- Often carries a negative or accusing tone.
- Unlike のに, くせに tends to imply criticism or blame.

### Examples

<details>
<summary>彼はお金がないくせに、よく買い物をする。</summary>

**Reading:** かれは おかねが ないくせに、よく かいものを する。

**Translation:** Even though he has no money, he shops a lot.

The speaker criticizes the contradiction.

</details>

<details>
<summary>子供のくせに、大人のような話し方をする。</summary>

**Reading:** こどもの くせに、おとなのような はなしかたを する。

**Translation:** Despite being a child, he talks like an adult.

</details>

## ～せい

### Form

- い-adjective + せい
- な-adjective + な + せい
- N + の + せい
- Plain form + せい

### Meaning / nuance

- **Because of… / due to…**
- Indicates a cause, usually one that leads to an undesirable result or is blamed for something.

### Usage

- Often followed by ～で, ～か, or a result clause.
- せいで directly attributes the result to the cause.
- せいか expresses uncertainty about the cause: “perhaps because…”

### Examples

<details>
<summary>雨のせいで、試合が中止になった。</summary>

**Reading:** あめの せいで、しあいが ちゅうしに なった。

**Translation:** The match was cancelled because of the rain.

せいで presents the rain as the cause of an undesirable result.

</details>

<details>
<summary>寝不足なせいか、今日は頭が痛い。</summary>

**Reading:** ねぶそくな せいか、きょうは あたまが いたい。

**Translation:** Perhaps because I didn't get enough sleep, I have a headache today.

せいか expresses uncertainty about the cause.

</details>

# Part F — Fixed constructions

## ～たとたんに

### Form

- Vた + とたんに
- Vた + とたん

### Meaning / nuance

- **The moment… / As soon as…**
- Describes something that happened immediately after another action or event.

### Usage

- The first action is usually completed before the second event occurs.
- Often describes an unexpected or uncontrollable event.
- The following event is generally not a deliberate action or command by the speaker.

### Examples

<details>
<summary>家を出たとたんに、雨が降り始めた。</summary>

**Reading:** いえを でたとたんに、あめが ふりはじめた。

**Translation:** The moment I left the house, it started raining.

とたんに emphasizes that the rain started immediately after leaving.

</details>

<details>
<summary>彼の顔を見たとたん、昔のことを思い出した。</summary>

**Reading:** かれの かおを みたとたん、むかしのことを おもいだした。

**Translation:** The moment I saw his face, I remembered the past.

</details>

## ～ほど

### Form

- N + ほど
- Vる + ほど
- い-adjective + ほど
- な-adjective + な + ほど

### Meaning / nuance

- **To the extent that… / so… that…**
- **About / approximately** when expressing quantity or degree.
- Indicates the degree or extent of something.

### Usage

- Can express that one thing increases or decreases in proportion to another.
- In comparisons, often appears in patterns such as ～ほど～ない.
- The specific meaning depends on the construction.

### Examples

<details>
<summary>立てないほど疲れている。</summary>

**Reading:** たてないほど つかれている。

**Translation:** I'm so tired that I can't stand.

ほど indicates the extreme degree of tiredness.

</details>

<details>
<summary>日本語は勉強すればするほど面白くなる。</summary>

**Reading:** にほんごは べんきょうすれば するほど おもしろくなる。

**Translation:** The more I study Japanese, the more interesting it becomes.

ほど expresses proportional change.

</details>

<details>
<summary>三時間ほど待ちました。</summary>

**Reading:** さんじかんほど まちました。

**Translation:** I waited for about three hours.

ほど means approximately.

</details>

## ～なら

### Form

- N + なら
- な-adjective + なら
- い-adjective + なら
- Plain form + なら

### Meaning / nuance

- **If… / If it's…**
- Sets a condition or takes something mentioned by the other person as the basis for a response.

### Usage

- Often used when responding to information or a topic already introduced.
- Can express advice, suggestions, or judgments based on that condition.
- N and な-adjectives can connect directly with なら.

### Examples

<details>
<summary>日本へ行くなら、京都もおすすめです。</summary>

**Reading:** にほんへ いくなら、きょうとも おすすめです。

**Translation:** If you're going to Japan, I also recommend Kyoto.

なら sets the condition for the recommendation.

</details>

<details>
<summary>時間があるなら、手伝ってください。</summary>

**Reading:** じかんが あるなら、てつだってください。

**Translation:** If you have time, please help me.

</details>

## ～た・だ + っけ・んだっけ

### Form

- Vた + っけ
- い-adjective + かったっけ
- な-adjective / N + だったっけ
- Plain form + んだっけ

### Meaning / nuance

- **Was it…? / What was… again?**
- Used when trying to remember or confirm something.

### Usage

- っけ is casual and often used in conversation or inner thoughts.
- んだっけ adds a feeling of recalling or checking the background of something.
- Can be used with past or present information, depending on the form.

### Examples

<details>
<summary>あの店、何時に閉まったっけ。</summary>

**Reading:** あの みせ、なんじに しまったっけ。

**Translation:** What time did that shop close again?

っけ expresses uncertainty while recalling information.

</details>

<details>
<summary>明日の会議は何時からなんだっけ。</summary>

**Reading:** あしたの かいぎは なんじから なんだっけ。

**Translation:** What time does tomorrow's meeting start again?

んだっけ is used to check or recall information.

</details>

## ～た・だ + って

### Form

- Vた + って
- い-adjective + かったって
- な-adjective / N + だったって
- Plain sentence + って

### Meaning / nuance

- **I heard that… / They said that…**
- Casual quotation or reported information.
- Can also mean **“even if…”** in certain constructions.

### Usage

- As reported information, って is a casual form of と or ということ.
- The reporting verb, such as 言っていた or 聞いた, is often omitted.
- In the meaning “even if,” it is often followed by a negative or contrasting statement.

### Examples

<details>
<summary>田中さん、昨日来なかったって。</summary>

**Reading:** たなかさん、きのう こなかったって。

**Translation:** I heard Tanaka didn't come yesterday.

って reports what someone said or what the speaker heard.

</details>

<details>
<summary>そんなこと、言ったって無駄だよ。</summary>

**Reading:** そんなこと、いったって むだだよ。

**Translation:** Even if you say something like that, it's useless.

って here is a casual form of ても.

</details>

## ～かわりに・～にかわって・～にかわり

### Form

- Vる / Vた + かわりに
- N + のかわりに
- N + にかわって
- N + にかわり
- N + にかわる + N

### Meaning / nuance

- **Instead of / in place of**
- **In exchange for / in return for**
- **On behalf of**

### Usage

- かわりに can indicate substitution or compensation.
- にかわって indicates that someone or something takes another's place.
- にかわり is a more formal or written form.
- にかわる + N means “a replacement for” or “something that takes the place of.”

### Examples

<details>
<summary>今日は車のかわりに電車で行く。</summary>

**Reading:** きょうは くるまの かわりに でんしゃで いく。

**Translation:** Today I'll go by train instead of by car.

かわりに indicates substitution.

</details>

<details>
<summary>父にかわって、私がご挨拶します。</summary>

**Reading:** ちちに かわって、わたしが ごあいさつします。

**Translation:** I will give the greeting on behalf of my father.

にかわって means “in place of.”

</details>

<details>
<summary>努力したかわりに、いい結果が得られた。</summary>

**Reading:** どりょくした かわりに、いい けっかが えられた。

**Translation:** In return for my efforts, I got a good result.

かわりに expresses a trade-off or compensation.

</details>

## ～おかげ

### Form

- N + のおかげで
- な-adjective + なおかげで
- Plain form + おかげで
- N + のおかげか

### Meaning / nuance

- **Thanks to… / owing to…**
- Expresses a cause that leads to a positive or welcome result.

### Usage

- Often used to express gratitude.
- Can be used with people, circumstances, or actions.
- おかげか expresses uncertainty about whether something was the cause.

### Examples

<details>
<summary>先生のおかげで、試験に合格できた。</summary>

**Reading:** せんせいの おかげで、しけんに ごうかくできた。

**Translation:** Thanks to my teacher, I was able to pass the exam.

おかげで presents the teacher's help as a positive cause.

</details>

<details>
<summary>よく寝たおかげか、今日は元気だ。</summary>

**Reading:** よく ねた おかげか、きょうは げんきだ。

**Translation:** Perhaps because I slept well, I feel energetic today.

おかげか expresses a possible positive cause.

</details>

## ～とおり・～どおり

### Form

- Vる / Vた + とおり
- N + のとおり
- N + どおり

### Meaning / nuance

- **As / according to / in the same way as**
- Indicates that something happens or is done in accordance with a plan, instruction, or expectation.

### Usage

- Vる + とおり often means “as one does / as instructed.”
- Vた + とおり means “as someone did / as something happened.”
- N + どおり is common with words such as 予定, 説明, and 計画.

### Examples

<details>
<summary>説明書のとおりに、組み立ててください。</summary>

**Reading:** せつめいしょの とおりに、くみたててください。

**Translation:** Please assemble it according to the instructions.

</details>

<details>
<summary>思ったとおり、彼は来なかった。</summary>

**Reading:** おもった とおり、かれは こなかった。

**Translation:** Just as I expected, he didn't come.

</details>

<details>
<summary>予定どおりに、会議が始まった。</summary>

**Reading:** よていどおりに、かいぎが はじまった。

**Translation:** The meeting started as scheduled.

</details>

## ～くらい・～ぐらい

### Form

- N + くらい / ぐらい
- Vる + くらい / ぐらい
- Plain form + くらい / ぐらい

### Meaning / nuance

- **About / approximately**
- **To the extent that…**
- Can express an approximate amount or an extreme degree.

### Usage

- くらい and ぐらい are generally interchangeable.
- With quantities, they mean “about.”
- With a clause, they can express the degree or extent of something.

### Examples

<details>
<summary>駅まで歩いて十分くらいです。</summary>

**Reading:** えきまで あるいて じゅっぷんくらいです。

**Translation:** It's about ten minutes on foot to the station.

くらい indicates an approximate amount.

</details>

<details>
<summary>泣きたいくらい、うれしかった。</summary>

**Reading:** なきたいくらい、うれしかった。

**Translation:** I was so happy that I wanted to cry.

くらい expresses an extreme degree.

</details>

## ～ばかり・～ばかりいる

### Form

- N + ばかり
- Vて + ばかりいる
- Vた + ばかり

### Meaning / nuance

- **Only / nothing but**
- **Keep doing… / do nothing but…**
- Vた + ばかり means **“just did…”**, referring to something that happened recently.

### Usage

- N + ばかり emphasizes that something is concentrated on one thing.
- Vて + ばかりいる often expresses criticism or dissatisfaction.
- Vた + ばかり describes a recent action; the actual time elapsed is subjective.

### Examples

<details>
<summary>最近、甘い物ばかり食べている。</summary>

**Reading:** さいきん、あまいものばかり たべている。

**Translation:** Recently, I've been eating nothing but sweets.

ばかり emphasizes excessive focus on one thing.

</details>

<details>
<summary>弟はゲームをしてばかりいる。</summary>

**Reading:** おとうとは ゲームを してばかりいる。

**Translation:** My younger brother does nothing but play games.

してばかりいる can express criticism.

</details>

<details>
<summary>日本に来たばかりです。</summary>

**Reading:** にほんに きたばかりです。

**Translation:** I just came to Japan.

たばかり indicates that the action happened recently.

</details>

## ～しかたがない・～しょうがない・～たまらない

### Form

- Vて + しかたがない / しょうがない
- い-adjective + くて + しかたがない / しょうがない
- な-adjective + で + しかたがない / しょうがない
- N + で + たまらない
- Vて + たまらない
- い-adjective + くて + たまらない
- な-adjective + で + たまらない

### Meaning / nuance

- **Can't help… / can't stand… / unbearably…**
- Expresses an intense feeling or sensation that is difficult to control.

### Usage

- しかたがない and しょうがない express that a feeling or situation cannot be helped.
- しょうがない is more conversational.
- たまらない emphasizes an overwhelming feeling or sensation.
- Often used with emotions, desires, pain, or physical sensations.

### Examples

<details>
<summary>試験の結果が気になってしかたがない。</summary>

**Reading:** しけんの けっかが きになって しかたがない。

**Translation:** I can't stop worrying about the exam results.

しかたがない expresses an uncontrollable feeling.

</details>

<details>
<summary>今日は暑くてしょうがない。</summary>

**Reading:** きょうは あつくて しょうがない。

**Translation:** It's unbearably hot today.

しょうがない is a casual equivalent of しかたがない.

</details>

<details>
<summary>彼に会いたくてたまらない。</summary>

**Reading:** かれに あいたくて たまらない。

**Translation:** I desperately want to see him.

たまらない emphasizes the intensity of the desire.

</details>
