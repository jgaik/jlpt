# A. Verb stem + auxiliary verbs

## ～始める・～終わる・～続ける

### Form

- Vます-stem + 始める
- Vます-stem + 終わる
- Vます-stem + 続ける

### Meaning / nuance

- **～始める** — begin doing something
- **～終わる** — finish doing something
- **～続ける** — continue doing something

These auxiliary verbs describe the beginning, completion, or continuation of an action.

### Usage

The main verb describes the action; the auxiliary verb describes its phase or duration.

### Examples

#### ～始める

<details>
<summary>雨が降り始めた。</summary>

**Reading:** あめがふりはじめた。

**Translation:** It started raining.

Emphasizes the moment an action or event begins.

</details>

#### ～終わる

<details>
<summary>宿題をやり終わった。</summary>

**Reading:** しゅくだいをやりおわった。

**Translation:** I finished doing my homework.

Indicates that the action has been completed.

</details>

#### ～続ける

<details>
<summary>彼は3時間も走り続けた。</summary>

**Reading:** かれはさんじかんもはしりつづけた。

**Translation:** He kept running for as long as three hours.

Emphasizes the continuation of an action over time. も adds emphasis to the unexpectedly long duration.

</details>

### Related grammar

- [[～出す]] — sudden beginning of an action
- [[～ている]] — ongoing action or resulting state

## ～そうもない・～そうにない・～そうにもない

### Form

- Vます-stem + そうにない
- Vます-stem + そうもない
- Vます-stem + そうにもない

### Meaning / nuance

Doesn't seem likely to… / Doesn't look like…

Expresses the speaker's judgment that something is unlikely to happen or be possible.

### Usage

Attached to the verb stem. Unlike ～そうだ (appearance), this construction expresses a negative prediction or expectation.

The three forms are very similar in meaning.

### Examples

<details>
<summary>雨は止みそうにない。</summary>

**Reading:** あめはやみそうにない。

**Translation:** It doesn't look like the rain will stop.

The speaker expects the rain to continue.

</details>

<details>
<summary>この量は一日では食べ終わりそうもない。</summary>

**Reading:** このりょうはいちにちではたべおわりそうもない。

**Translation:** It doesn't seem like I'll finish eating this amount in one day.

そうもない emphasizes the negative expectation.

</details>

<details>
<summary>彼はまだ帰ってきそうにもない。</summary>

**Reading:** かれはまだかえってきそうにもない。

**Translation:** It doesn't look like he's going to come back anytime soon.

にもない can add emphasis to the sense that something is unlikely.

</details>

### Related grammar

- [[～そうだ]] — looks like / seems (appearance)
- [[～らしい]] — apparently / seems to be
- [[～はずがない]] — there is no way that…

## ～きる・～きれない

### Form

- Vます-stem + きる
- Vます-stem + きれない

### Meaning / nuance

- **～きる** — to do something completely / to finish all of something
- **～きれない** — to be unable to finish or do something completely

Emphasizes reaching the end or limit of an action.

### Usage

Often used with actions involving quantity, duration, or effort.

～きれない commonly expresses that something is too much to finish or handle.

### Examples

<details>
<summary>マラソンを最後まで走りきった。</summary>

**Reading:** マラソンをさいごまではしりきった。

**Translation:** I ran the marathon all the way to the end.

きる emphasizes completing the entire action.

</details>

<details>
<summary>一日では読みきれないほど本がある。</summary>

**Reading:** いちにちではよみきれないほどほんがある。

**Translation:** There are so many books that I can't finish reading them in one day.

きれない expresses that the quantity exceeds what can be completed.

</details>

<details>
<summary>感謝してもしきれない。</summary>

**Reading:** かんしゃしてもしきれない。

**Translation:** I can't express enough gratitude.

A fixed expression meaning that gratitude is too great to fully express.

</details>

### Related grammar

- [[～終わる]] — finish an action
- [[～っこない]] — there is no way to…
